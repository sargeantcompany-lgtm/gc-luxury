Adam Sargeant

Prestige & Luxury Property Specialist — Gold Coast

Some agents know the Gold Coast waterfront. Adam Sargeant was born into it.

His ancestor Thomas Ainsworth arrived on the NSW North Coast in 1847 as a shipwright — navigating the same coastal rivers and waterways that defined the region's earliest trade and settlement. Lake Ainsworth near Lennox Head bears the family name to this day. The water has always been in the blood.

Adam has spent decades in real estate — owning and managing agencies, operating at the top of the market with an energy and relentlessness that simply doesn't quit. With close to ten years working with Ray White, he understands the systems and infrastructure behind consistently successful sales outcomes, and brings a background in technology that ensures every listing performs at its peak across every digital channel.

Over the course of his career Adam has been trusted to host and work alongside some of the most recognised names on the planet.

Beyond the Commission

Three years volunteering with the Starlight Children's Foundation, supporting refugee housing and directing real estate resources toward the rescue of children caught in trafficking — when Adam says he's in this for more than a commission, the record backs it up.

He has now joined the team at Ray White Malan + Co, specialising in prestige and luxury property across the Gold Coast's most tightly held addresses — waterfront, canal front and everything the top end of this market has to offer.
