Why auction works

  1. Commitment

    Your property is put up for sale on a specified date, preceded by a high-impact marketing campaign that shows your commitment to selling — crucial in the current market.

The Summer Auction Experience

On Saturday 30 January 2027 we host our annual Summer Auction Experience at The Star Gold Coast. This property showcase is renowned for exceptional results, with a host of Gold Coast homes sold under the hammer. The event is held in room and online, so everyone can take part.

The influx of interstate buyers continues, with Queensland recording the highest inbound domestic migration in the country over the past 12 months. Our market fundamentals remain strong, boosted every January by interstate travellers and investors looking to buy.

Buyers want a lifestyle of sunshine and security, and there's no better place than the Gold Coast to deliver it. Backed by a well-orchestrated marketing campaign, sellers in the Summer Auction Experience benefit from more publicity and exposure to more buyers.

FAQ

Your questions, answered

Why sell at auction and not private treaty?

Read more

Data suggests selling under the hammer secures clients 11% more than selling privately. With far more bidders on hand to create healthy competition, auction is the best way to obtain a premium price.

Is it better to auction on-site or in-room?

Read more

Both have their merits. An on-site auction lets neighbours attend, creating a high-stakes, competitive atmosphere. An in-room auction lets us control the environment for a highly strategic auction. On-site auctions held during the week of the Summer Auction Experience receive all the same marketing benefits as the main event.

How will the event raise my property's profile?

Read more

With so many properties for sale on the Gold Coast, yours needs to stand out. Our tailored auction marketing campaign puts it in the spotlight, increasing competition and attracting multiple buyers.

When will I set the reserve price?

Read more

The reserve is set just before the Summer Auction Experience. We still encourage offers throughout the campaign to keep a close eye on perceived value, and we'll guide you to a fully informed decision on your reserve.

Can I pass in if I don't get my price?

Read more

Absolutely. If that happens, the highest bidder is usually invited to negotiate with you to see if a sale can still be made. You're in complete control and make the final decision on price.

My home isn't high-end — is it worth it?

Read more

Yes. From first homes and family homes to villas, luxury estates and even house-and-land developments, we sell them all at the Summer Auction. Our marketing strategies are tailored to suit any budget.

Can I attend online or in person?

Read more

Both. The event is accessible to everyone, in room and online.
