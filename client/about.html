Heritage & Purpose

A deep personal connection to Australian waters

Underpinning his professional drive is a deep personal connection to Australian waters. His family's legacy dates back to 1847, when his ancestor, shipwright Thomas Ainsworth, first navigated the region's coastal rivers—a heritage honored today by Lake Ainsworth. Grounded by perspective and committed to purpose, Adam donates his time to the Starlight Children's Foundation and directs real estate resources toward child anti-trafficking causes.
