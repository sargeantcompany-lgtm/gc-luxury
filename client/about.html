The Story

The water has always been in the blood

His family's connection to Australian waterways dates back to 1847, when his ancestor, shipwright Thomas Ainsworth, first navigated the coastal rivers of the region—a legacy reflected today in Lake Ainsworth. That deep, instinctual understanding of waterfront living forms the foundation of Adam's client-first approach across the Gold Coast's most prestigious enclaves.

With over two decades of real estate leadership—including owning and managing agencies and a tenured decade within the Ray White network—Adam brings a rare, steady hand to high-stakes property transactions.
