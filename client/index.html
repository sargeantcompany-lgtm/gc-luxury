Beyond the Basics: The Buyer Database

How useful an agent's buyer database is depends on a few factors

90day

Is it kept up to date?

Ray White runs a 90-day buyer match to keep things current.

?

Are the buyers qualified, and have they already made offers?

If a buyer has been on a database for a while without buying, you have to wonder why. Often, especially in this market, it's because they aren't prepared to make an acceptable offer.

5–10

So how useful is an agent's database really? Unless a buyer is looking for a specific property, the agent needs a strong relationship with them for the database to be worth much. In the luxury price range, an agent might have only five to ten truly qualified buyers, and they hold them tight, often because those buyers may be selling their own homes. Beyond that, most databases are made up of email enquiries and open home attendees. Those buyers appear on most agents' lists and are searching online regularly anyway.
