Two Ways to Sell

Private Treaty

Balances the desire for a quick sale with maximising buyer interest and offers. Suits sellers who are comfortable with a clear price and where timing isn't pressing — priced right, so the buyers who'll stretch 10–15% aren't lost before they see the home.

A focused campaign that brings every buyer to one place at one time, with no price ceiling and a set date that makes buyers act. You set the reserve, and once it's met the sale is unconditional under the hammer.
